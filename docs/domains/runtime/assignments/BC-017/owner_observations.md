## O-01 — v0.7.4.1 heuristic feel and reliability boundary

Dad identifies v0.7.4.1 as the historical version in which Blu had the
strongest, most natural heuristic feel and was highly useful. Dad does not
identify it as the most reliable or stable version. Dad explicitly reports that
pre-Exec v0.7.4 hallucinated and drifted easily, and that Exec later improved
Blu's reliability and control.

### Archive-derived evidence boundary

The archive independently shows unusually dense Persona, Anchors, and Teaching
guidance in v0.7.4.1. That source finding is separate from Dad's observation and
does not establish a causal explanation for the version's heuristic feel.

## O-02 — School / classroom era

Dad remembers the School / classroom system as strong and useful approximately
during the v0.8.x through v0.13.x era. It was not perfect. Dad remembers that it
was eventually abandoned while development focus shifted toward stabilizing
Blu's heuristics.

### Archive-derived evidence boundary

The archive independently supports School's existence, explicit scaffolding,
later ownership changes, and removal or archival during rebuilds. Without
runtime telemetry, it cannot establish experienced quality or progressive
degradation.
